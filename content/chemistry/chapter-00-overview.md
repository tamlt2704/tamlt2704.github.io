# Chemistry - Course Overview

## Welcome to Chemistry!

Chemistry is the study of matter — what it's made of, how it behaves, and how it transforms.

## Course Structure

| Chapter | Topic                 | Key Concepts                         |
| ------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 1       | Atoms & Elements      | Atomic structure, isotopes           |
| 2       | Periodic Table        | Groups, periods, trends              |
| 3       | Chemical Bonds        | Ionic, covalent, metallic            |
| 4       | Reactions & Equations | Types, balancing                     |
| 5       | Stoichiometry         | Mole calculations, limiting reagents |
| 6       | Acids, Bases & pH     | pH scale, neutralization             |
| 7       | Organic Chemistry     | Carbon compounds, functional groups  |

## Prerequisites

- Basic math (ratios, scientific notation)
- Understanding of measurement and units

## Key Skills You'll Develop

- Balancing chemical equations
- Predicting products of reactions
- Calculating quantities in reactions
- Understanding molecular structure
