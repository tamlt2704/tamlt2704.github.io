# Physics Fundamentals - Course Overview

## Welcome to Physics!

Physics is the study of how the universe works — from the smallest particles to the largest galaxies.

## Course Structure

| Chapter | Topic                   | Key Concepts                           |
| ------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 1       | Motion & Kinematics     | Displacement, velocity, acceleration   |
| 2       | Forces & Newton's Laws  | Force, mass, inertia, action-reaction  |
| 3       | Energy & Work           | Kinetic/potential energy, conservation |
| 4       | Waves & Sound           | Frequency, wavelength, resonance       |
| 5       | Electricity & Magnetism | Charge, current, fields                |
| 6       | Thermodynamics          | Heat, entropy, laws of thermodynamics  |
| 7       | Modern Physics          | Relativity, quantum mechanics intro    |

## Prerequisites

- Basic algebra (solving equations, graphing)
- Trigonometry fundamentals (sin, cos, tan)
- Scientific notation and unit conversions

## Problem-Solving Strategy

```
1. READ the problem carefully
2. DRAW a diagram
3. IDENTIFY knowns and unknowns
4. SELECT appropriate equations
5. SOLVE algebraically, then plug in numbers
6. CHECK units and reasonableness
```
