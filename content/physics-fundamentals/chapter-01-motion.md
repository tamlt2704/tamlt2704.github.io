# Chapter 1: Motion & Kinematics

## Key Concepts

### Displacement vs Distance

- **Distance**: total path length (scalar)
- **Displacement**: change in position (vector): Δx = x_f - x_i

### Velocity & Acceleration

```
v_avg = Δx / Δt
a_avg = Δv / Δt
```

## Kinematic Equations (Constant Acceleration)

| #   | Equation        | Missing |
| --- | --------------- | ------- |
| 1   | v = v₀ + at     | Δx      |
| 2   | Δx = v₀t + ½at² | v       |
| 3   | v² = v₀² + 2aΔx | t       |
| 4   | Δx = ½(v₀ + v)t | a       |

## Free Fall

```
g = 9.8 m/s² (downward)
Convention: up = positive → a = -9.8 m/s²
```

## Projectile Motion

```
        . * * .
      *         *       Horizontal: vₓ = v₀cosθ (constant)
    *             *     Vertical:   vᵧ = v₀sinθ - gt
   *       H       *
  *                  *
 *______ R ___________*

Range: R = v₀² sin(2θ) / g
Max range at θ = 45°
```

## Real-World Example: Car Braking

A car at 25 m/s brakes at -5 m/s². Stopping distance?

```
v² = v₀² + 2aΔx
0 = 625 + 2(-5)Δx → Δx = 62.5 m
```

## Practice Problems

**P1.** A train accelerates from rest at 2 m/s² for 10 s. Find final velocity and distance.

> **Solution:**
> v = 0 + 2(10) = 20 m/s
> Δx = 0 + ½(2)(100) = 100 m

**P2.** A ball thrown up at 30 m/s. Find max height and total air time.

> **Solution:**
> h = v₀²/(2g) = 900/19.6 = 45.9 m
> t_total = 2v₀/g = 60/9.8 = 6.12 s

**P3.** Projectile launched at 50 m/s, 30° above horizontal. Find range.

> **Solution:**
> R = 2500 × sin60° / 9.8 = 2500(0.866)/9.8 = 221 m

**P4.** Car A moves at 20 m/s constant. Car B starts from rest, accelerates at 4 m/s². When does B catch A?

> **Solution:**
> 20t = ½(4)t² → 20 = 2t → t = 10 s
