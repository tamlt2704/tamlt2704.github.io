# Chapter 6: Thermodynamics & Modern Physics

## Part A: Thermodynamics

### Heat & Temperature

- **Temperature**: average kinetic energy of particles
- **Heat (Q)**: energy transferred due to temperature difference

```
Q = mcΔT    (specific heat equation)
```

| Substance | c (J/kg·K) |
| --------- | ---------- |
| Water     | 4186       |
| Iron      | 450        |
| Aluminum  | 900        |

### Laws of Thermodynamics

**1st Law:** ΔU = Q - W (energy conservation)
**2nd Law:** Entropy always increases; heat flows hot→cold
**3rd Law:** Absolute zero (0 K) is unattainable

### Ideal Gas Law

```
PV = nRT    (R = 8.314 J/mol·K)
```

### Heat Engines

```
Hot (T_H) → Q_H → [Engine] → W out
                      ↓ Q_C
                  Cold (T_C)

Efficiency: η = 1 - T_C/T_H  (Carnot max)
```

## Part B: Modern Physics

### Special Relativity (Einstein, 1905)

Postulates: (1) Laws of physics same in all inertial frames, (2) c = 3×10⁸ m/s is constant

```
Time dilation:   Δt = γΔt₀        (moving clocks run slow)
Length contract: L = L₀/γ          (moving objects shorter)
Mass-energy:     E = mc²

γ = 1/√(1 - v²/c²)
```

### Quantum Mechanics Intro

**Photoelectric Effect:**

```
E = hf = hc/λ     (h = 6.63×10⁻³⁴ J·s)
KE_max = hf - φ   (φ = work function)
```

**Wave-Particle Duality:**

```
de Broglie: λ = h/(mv)
```

**Uncertainty Principle:**

```
Δx · Δp ≥ h/(4π)
```

### Atomic Energy Levels

```
    ─── n=3  (-1.51 eV)
    ─── n=2  (-3.4 eV)
    ─── n=1  (-13.6 eV)  Ground state

Photon emitted: E = E_high - E_low = hf
```

## Practice Problems

**P1.** Heat 2 kg water from 20°C to 100°C. Energy needed?

> **Solution:**
> Q = mcΔT = 2(4186)(80) = 669,760 J ≈ 670 kJ

**P2.** Carnot engine: T_H=600K, T_C=300K. Max efficiency?

> **Solution:**
> η = 1 - 300/600 = 50%

**P3.** Find energy of a photon with λ = 500 nm.

> **Solution:**
> E = hc/λ = (6.63×10⁻³⁴)(3×10⁸)/(500×10⁻⁹) = 3.98×10⁻¹⁹ J = 2.49 eV

**P4.** Spaceship at 0.8c. Find γ and time dilation for 10-year trip (crew time).

> **Solution:**
> γ = 1/√(1-0.64) = 1/0.6 = 1.667
> Earth time for 10 ly at 0.8c = 12.5 years
> Crew time = 12.5/1.667 = 7.5 years

**P5.** How much energy from converting 1 g of mass? (E=mc²)

> **Solution:**
> E = 0.001 × (3×10⁸)² = 9×10¹³ J = 90 TJ
