## The Clock ⏰

A clock has TWO hands:

- **Short hand** = the HOUR (what time it is)
- **Long hand** = the minutes

When the long hand points UP (to 12), it's **o'clock**!

---

## O'Clock Times 🕐🕑🕒

- 🕐 **1 o'clock** — short hand on 1!
- 🕑 **2 o'clock** — short hand on 2!
- 🕒 **3 o'clock** — short hand on 3!
- 🕓 **4 o'clock** — short hand on 4!
- 🕔 **5 o'clock** — short hand on 5!
- 🕕 **6 o'clock** — short hand on 6!

The long hand ALWAYS points to 12 for o'clock! ☝️

## Days of the Week 🎵

Sing it! 🎤

**Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday!**

- **Yesterday** = the day BEFORE today
- **Today** = RIGHT NOW! 📍
- **Tomorrow** = the day AFTER today

What day is it today? What was yesterday? What's tomorrow? 🤔

## Let's Learn Coins! 🪙

- 🪙 **Penny** = 1 cent (the small copper one!)
- 🪙 **Nickel** = 5 cents (bigger, smooth edge!)
- 🪙 **Dime** = 10 cents (tiny but worth more!)
- 🪙 **Quarter** = 25 cents (the big one!)

---

## Counting Coins 💰

Count by **1s** with pennies: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5!

Count by **5s** with nickels: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25!

Count by **10s** with dimes: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50!

---

## 🎲 Game Time!

**What Time Is It, Mr. Wolf?** 🐺

One person is the wolf. They say a time ("3 o'clock!"). Everyone takes 3 steps forward! When the wolf says "DINNER TIME!" — run! 🏃

**Playing Shop** 🏪

Set up a pretend store! Put price tags on toys (1 cent, 5 cents, 10 cents). Use real or pretend coins to buy things!

"This teddy costs 5 cents. Can you find the right coin?" 🧸

**Clock Matching** ⏰

Draw clocks showing different o'clock times. Can you match the clock to the number?

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Tip

Use an analog clock at home and reference it throughout the day: "It's 3 o'clock — snack time!" For coins, let your child handle real money (supervised). Playing shop is one of the best ways to learn coin values. Don't worry about telling time beyond o'clock at this age — half-past and minutes come later! 🌈
