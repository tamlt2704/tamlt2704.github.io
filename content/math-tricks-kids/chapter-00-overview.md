## 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Tip

Read one chapter at a time. Let your child lead — if they want to skip ahead or repeat, that's great! Use real objects (toys, snacks, fingers) to make the math real. Celebrate effort, not just correct answers. Keep sessions short (10-15 minutes) and always end on a positive note! 🌈
