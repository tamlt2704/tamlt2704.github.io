**2** ... **4** ... **6** ... **8** ... **10**!

## Counting With Fingers ✋

Your hands are counting tools!

✋ = 5 fingers on one hand

✋✋ = 10 fingers on two hands!

Hold up 3 fingers. That's 3!

Hold up 7 fingers. That's 7!

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Tip

Practice counting with everyday things: stairs, grapes, toys during cleanup. Count out loud together often — in the car, at the store, during bath time. Let your child touch each object as they count (one-to-one correspondence is key at this age!). 🌈
