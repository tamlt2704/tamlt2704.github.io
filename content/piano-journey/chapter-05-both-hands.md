## The Challenge

Playing both hands together is the hardest skill for beginners. Your brain must coordinate two independent patterns simultaneously. The secret: **start absurdly slow**.

## Method: Hand-Over-Hand

1. Learn right hand perfectly (from memory)
2. Learn left hand perfectly (from memory)
3. Play both at **half speed** — focus on where they align
4. Gradually speed up

## 🎵 Song 1: Twinkle Twinkle (Both Hands)

```
Bar 1:
  RH: | C   C   G   G |
  LH: | C───────────── |  (hold C-E-G chord)
       1   2   3   4

Bar 2:
  RH: | A   A   G   - |
  LH: | C───────────── |  (same chord)
       1   2   3   4

Bar 3:
  RH: | F   F   E   E |
  LH: | F───────────── |  (F-A-C chord)
       1   2   3   4

Bar 4:
  RH: | D   D   C   - |
  LH: | C───────────── |  (C-E-G chord)
       1   2   3   4
```

The left hand just holds one chord per bar. Focus on keeping steady time.

## The 4-Step Practice Loop

For each 2-bar section:

```
Step 1: RH alone (5 times perfectly)
Step 2: LH alone (5 times perfectly)
Step 3: Both hands, VERY slow (metronome at 40 BPM)
Step 4: Gradually increase speed (add 5 BPM each day)
```

## Troubleshooting

| Problem                      | Solution                                         |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Hands go at different speeds | Use a metronome, count out loud                  |
| Left hand forgets            | Practice LH alone more — it needs extra time     |
| Tension in shoulders         | Stop, shake out, breathe, restart                |
| Can't coordinate             | Simplify LH to single notes instead of chords    |
| Losing place                 | Mark the bar where you get lost, drill that spot |
