## Left Hand Position

```
Left Hand on: C  D  E  F  G
Finger:       5  4  3  2  1
```

Note: left hand finger 5 (pinky) starts on C, thumb (1) on G. Opposite of right hand!

## Exercise 1: Mirror of Right Hand

Play the same 5-note patterns with left hand:

```
| C D E F G | G F E D C |
| 5 4 3 2 1 | 1 2 3 4 5 |
```

Repeat 10 times. It will feel awkward — that's normal!

## Basic Chords (Left Hand)

A chord = 3+ notes played together. The three most important chords in C major:

```
C Major:  C - E - G  (fingers 5 - 3 - 1)
F Major:  F - A - C  (fingers 5 - 3 - 1)
G Major:  G - B - D  (fingers 5 - 3 - 1)
```

These three chords accompany hundreds of songs!

## Exercise 2: Chord Practice

```
| C chord | C chord | F chord | F chord |
| C-E-G   | C-E-G   | F-A-C   | F-A-C   |

| G chord | G chord | C chord | C chord |
| G-B-D   | G-B-D   | C-E-G   | C-E-G   |
```

Play each chord as a block (all 3 notes together). Hold for 4 beats.

## Exercise 3: Broken Chords (Arpeggios)

Instead of playing all notes together, play them one at a time:

```
C chord broken: | C   E   G   E |
Fingers:        | 5   3   1   3 |

F chord broken: | F   A   C   A |
G chord broken: | G   B   D   B |
```

## 🎵 Song: Twinkle Twinkle (Left Hand Accompaniment)

```
Right Hand: | C C G G | A A G - | F F E E | D D C - |
Left Hand:  | C-E-G   | C-E-G   | F-A-C   | C-E-G   |
             (hold)    (hold)    (hold)    (hold)
```

For now, just play one chord per bar (hold it for 4 beats).

## 🎵 Song: Mary Had a Little Lamb (Left Hand Only)

```
| C - - - | C - - - | G - - - | C - - - |
| 5       | 5       | 1       | 5       |
```

Just single bass notes — one per bar. Simple but effective!

## Common Mistakes

- **Flat fingers** — keep them curved!
- **Tension** — shake out your hand if it feels tight
- **Rushing** — left hand is always slower to develop, be patient
- **Wrong fingers** — always use the correct fingering (5-3-1 for chords)
