## 🎵 Learning Für Elise (Step by Step)

### Section A (the famous part):

```
RH: | E D# E D# E | B D C A - |
    | 1 2  1 2  1 | 5 4 3 1   |

    | C E A B - | E G# B C - |
    | 1 3 5 4   | 1 3  5 4   |

    | E D# E D# E | B D C A - |
```

### Left Hand (Section A):

```
LH: | A───── | A-E-A | E-G#-B |
    | (rest) | 5-3-1 | 5-3-1  |
```

### Practice Plan (4 weeks):

- **Week 1**: RH Section A only, memorize
- **Week 2**: LH Section A, then combine
- **Week 3**: Section B (the contrasting middle)
- **Week 4**: Connect A-B-A, add dynamics

## The Pedal

The right pedal (sustain) holds notes after you release keys:

```
Rules:
1. Press pedal AFTER playing the chord (not before)
2. Change pedal on each new chord
3. Technique: "play - press - play - lift-press"

Common mistake: holding pedal too long → muddy sound
```

## Practice Structure (45 min)

```
0-10 min:  Scales (2 keys, hands together, 2 octaves)
10-15 min: Technique exercise (Hanon, Czerny, or arpeggios)
15-35 min: Current piece (focus on hardest section)
35-40 min: Sight-reading (new easy piece, first attempt)
40-45 min: Play something fun (any piece you already know)
```
