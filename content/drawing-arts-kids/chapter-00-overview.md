# Drawing & Arts for Kids - Course Overview

## Welcome, Young Artist! 🎨

You're about to learn how to draw amazing things! The secret? Everything starts with simple shapes. If you can draw a circle, a square, and a triangle — you can draw ANYTHING!

## What You'll Learn

| Chapter | Topic                | What You'll Make                 |
| ------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 1       | Basic Shapes & Lines | Patterns, designs                |
| 2       | Drawing Animals      | Cats, dogs, birds, fish          |
| 3       | Drawing People       | Faces, stick figures, characters |
| 4       | Colors & Shading     | 3D effects, mood                 |
| 5       | Landscapes & Scenes  | Trees, houses, nature            |
| 6       | Fun Projects         | Cards, comics, posters           |

## What You'll Need

- Paper (any kind!)
- Pencils
- Eraser
- Colored pencils, crayons, or markers
- Your imagination! ✨

## Tips for Success

1. **There are no mistakes in art** — only happy accidents!
2. **Practice makes progress** — draw a little every day
3. **Start light** — press gently with your pencil first
4. **Have fun!** — art is about expressing yourself

## For Parents & Teachers

This course is designed for ages 5-12. Younger children may need help reading instructions. Each chapter has 3-5 exercises that progress from simple to more detailed.

Let's get started! 🖍️
