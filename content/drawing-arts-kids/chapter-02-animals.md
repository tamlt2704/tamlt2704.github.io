# Chapter 2: Drawing Animals 🐾

## The Secret: Animals Are Made of Shapes!

Every animal starts with circles, ovals, and triangles. Let's learn step by step!

---

## Exercise 1: Draw a Cat 🐱

**Step 1:** Draw a circle (head) and an oval below it (body)
**Step 2:** Add two triangles on top (ears)
**Step 3:** Add a curved tail
**Step 4:** Draw face details

```

**Step 2:** Add floppy ear shapes (half ovals)

## Exercise 5: Draw a Butterfly 🦋

**Step 1:** Draw a thin oval (body)
**Step 2:** Add 4 wing shapes (big ovals on sides)
**Step 3:** Add antennae (lines with dots)
**Step 4:** Decorate wings with circles and patterns!

```

## Tips for Drawing Animals

- Start with **light pencil lines** for the shapes
- **Erase** the shape lines after you add details
- Look at real photos for inspiration
- Every animal has a **unique shape** — observe what makes it special!
