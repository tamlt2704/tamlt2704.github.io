The Staff

Music is written on 5 lines called a staff:

Treble Clef (Right Hand): ─────── F ─── (5th line) ─────── D ─── (4th line) ─────── B ─── (3rd line) ─────── G ─── (2nd line) ─────── E ─── (1st line) Spaces (bottom to top): F - A - C - E

Memory tricks:

  • Lines: Every Good Boy Does Fine
  • Spaces: F-A-C-E (spells "face")

Bass Clef (Left Hand)

─────── A ─── (5th line) ─────── F ─── (4th line) ─────── D ─── (3rd line) ─────── B ─── (2nd line) ─────── G ─── (1st line) Spaces: A - C - E - G

Memory tricks:

  • Lines: Good Boys Do Fine Always
  • Spaces: All Cows Eat Grass

Middle C

Middle C sits on a small line (ledger line) between the two staves:

Treble: ─────────────── ─────────────── ─────────────── ─────────────── ─────────────── ─── C ─── ← ledger line ─────────────── Bass: ─────────────── ─────────────── ─────────────── ───────────────

Note Values (Duration)

SymbolNameBeats
𝅝Whole note4 beats
𝅗𝅥Half note2 beats
♩Quarter note1 beat
♪Eighth note½ beat

Counting

In 4/4 time (most common):

| 1 2 3 4 | 1 2 3 4 | | ♩ ♩ ♩ ♩ | 𝅗𝅥 𝅗𝅥 | | C D E F | G─────── E─────── |

Count "1, 2, 3, 4" steadily like a clock ticking.

Practice Task

  1. Write out the treble clef line notes (E, G, B, D, F) on paper
  2. Write out the space notes (F, A, C, E)
  3. On the piano, play each note as you say its name
  4. Use a flashcard app or draw cards with notes — quiz yourself daily
