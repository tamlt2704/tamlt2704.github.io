The Path

Beginner (Months 1-6) → Posture, note reading, simple melodies → Songs: Twinkle Twinkle, Mary Had a Little Lamb Elementary (Months 6-18) → Both hands, basic chords, rhythm → Songs: Ode to Joy, Für Elise (simplified) Intermediate (Years 2-4) → Scales, arpeggios, dynamics, pedal → Songs: Moonlight Sonata (mvt 1), River Flows in You Advanced (Years 4-8) → Complex repertoire, interpretation, performance → Songs: Chopin Nocturnes, Debussy, Bach Inventions Professional (Years 8+) → Concert repertoire, sight-reading, improvisation

Daily Practice Structure

LevelDurationBreakdown
Beginner15-20 min5 min warm-up, 10 min piece, 5 min review
Elementary30 min10 min scales, 15 min pieces, 5 min sight-reading
Intermediate45-60 min15 min technique, 30 min repertoire, 15 min new material
Advanced90-120 min20 min scales/arpeggios, 60 min repertoire, 20 min sight-reading, 20 min theory

The Golden Rules

  1. Slow is fast — practice slowly until perfect, then speed up
  2. Hands separate first — always learn each hand alone before combining
  3. Small sections — work on 2-4 bars at a time, not the whole piece
  4. Daily > long sessions — 20 min every day beats 2 hours on weekends
  5. Listen — listen to recordings of pieces you're learning
