The Challenge

Playing both hands together is the hardest skill for beginners. Your brain must coordinate two independent patterns simultaneously. The secret: start absurdly slow.

Method: Hand-Over-Hand

  1. Learn right hand perfectly (from memory)
  2. Learn left hand perfectly (from memory)
  3. Play both at half speed — focus on where they align
  4. Gradually speed up

🎵 Song 1: Twinkle Twinkle (Both Hands)

Bar 1: RH: | C C G G | LH: | C───────────── | (hold C-E-G chord) 1 2 3 4 Bar 2: RH: | A A G - | LH: | C───────────── | (same chord) 1 2 3 4 Bar 3: RH: | F F E E | LH: | F───────────── | (F-A-C chord) 1 2 3 4 Bar 4: RH: | D D C - | LH: | C───────────── | (C-E-G chord) 1 2 3 4

The left hand just holds one chord per bar. Focus on keeping steady time.

🎵 Song 2: Ode to Joy (Both Hands)

Bar 1-2: RH: | E E F G | G F E D | LH: | C─────── | G─────── | Bar 3-4: RH: | C C D E | E - D D | LH: | C─────── | G─────── |

Left hand plays single bass notes (C or G), one per bar.

The 4-Step Practice Loop

For each 2-bar section:

Step 1: RH alone (5 times perfectly) Step 2: LH alone (5 times perfectly) Step 3: Both hands, VERY slow (metronome at 40 BPM) Step 4: Gradually increase speed (add 5 BPM each day)

Troubleshooting

ProblemSolution
Hands go at different speedsUse a metronome, count out loud
Left hand forgetsPractice LH alone more — it needs extra time
Tension in shouldersStop, shake out, breathe, restart
Can't coordinateSimplify LH to single notes instead of chords
Losing placeMark the bar where you get lost, drill that spot
