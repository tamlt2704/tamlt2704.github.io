The pattern repeats: 2 black keys, 3 black keys, 2 black keys, 3 black keys...

Find Middle C: it's the C closest to the center of the keyboard (usually has a brand logo above it).

Finger Numbers

Left Hand: Right Hand: 5 4 3 2 1 1 2 3 4 5 │ │ │ │ │ │ │ │ │ │ pinky → thumb thumb → pinky

Both thumbs are 1. Pinkies are 5. This numbering is used in all sheet music.

Sitting Posture

  1. Bench height — forearms parallel to the floor when hands are on keys
  2. Distance — elbows slightly in front of your body
  3. Feet — flat on the floor (use a footstool for kids)
  4. Back — straight but relaxed, not stiff
  5. Wrists — level with the keyboard, not drooping or raised
  6. Fingers — curved like holding a ball, fingertips on keys

Hand Position

Imagine holding a small orange:

  • Fingers curved naturally
  • Knuckles slightly raised (not flat)
  • Thumb on its side (not flat on the key)
  • Relaxed — no tension in wrists or shoulders

First Exercise: Finding C-D-E-F-G

Place your right hand thumb (1) on Middle C:

Finger: 1 2 3 4 5 Note: C D E F G

Press each key gently, one at a time. Say the note name as you play.

Practice Task

  1. Find all the C notes on your keyboard (there are 8!)
  2. Play C-D-E-F-G with right hand, saying each note
  3. Play G-F-E-D-C going back down
  4. Repeat 5 times, slowly
