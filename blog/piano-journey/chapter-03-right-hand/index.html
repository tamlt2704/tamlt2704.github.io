Position: C Major (5-finger)

Right Hand on: C D E F G Finger: 1 2 3 4 5

All songs in this chapter use only these 5 notes!

🎵 Song 1: Twinkle Twinkle Little Star

| C C G G | A A G - | F F E E | D D C - | | 1 1 5 5 | 6 6 5 - | 4 4 3 3 | 2 2 1 - | | G G F F | E E D - | G G F F | E E D - | | 5 5 4 4 | 3 3 2 - | 5 5 4 4 | 3 3 2 - | | C C G G | A A G - | F F E E | D D C - |

Finger numbers: (A = move finger 1 up to A, or use finger 6 concept — just stretch)

Practice tips:

  • Play very slowly, one note per second
  • Say the note names out loud
  • Once comfortable, try singing along

🎵 Song 5: Jingle Bells (Chorus)

| E E E - | E E E - | E G C D | E - - - | | 3 3 3 - | 3 3 3 - | 3 5 1 2 | 3 - - - | | F F F F | F E E E | E D D E | D - G - | | 4 4 4 4 | 4 3 3 3 | 3 2 2 3 | 2 - 5 - |

Practice Method for Each Song

  1. Clap the rhythm — clap the beat pattern before playing
  2. Right hand alone — play slowly, correct notes only
  3. Small sections — learn 2 bars at a time
  4. Loop trouble spots — repeat difficult bars 10 times
  5. Connect sections — join 2-bar chunks together
  6. Full piece — play start to finish without stopping
  7. Speed up — gradually increase tempo

How Slow is Slow Enough?

If you make a mistake, you're going too fast. The right tempo is where you can play every note correctly without hesitation.
