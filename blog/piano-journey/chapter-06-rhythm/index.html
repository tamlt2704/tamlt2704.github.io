Time Signatures

The two numbers at the start of a piece:

4/4 = 4 beats per bar, quarter note = 1 beat (most common) 3/4 = 3 beats per bar (waltz) 6/8 = 6 beats per bar, eighth note = 1 beat (compound) 2/4 = 2 beats per bar (march)

Note Durations in 4/4

𝅝 Whole note = 4 beats | 1─────2─────3─────4───── | 𝅗𝅥 Half note = 2 beats | 1─────2───── | 3─────4───── | ♩ Quarter note = 1 beat | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | ♪ Eighth note = ½ beat | 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & | ♬ Sixteenth note = ¼ beat | 1 e & a 2 e & a 3 e & a 4 e & a |

Dotted Notes

A dot adds half the note's value:

  • Dotted half note = 3 beats (2 + 1)
  • Dotted quarter = 1.5 beats (1 + 0.5)

Rests (Silence)

Every note has a matching rest:

  • Whole rest = 4 beats silence
  • Half rest = 2 beats silence
  • Quarter rest = 1 beat silence

Rhythm Exercise 1: Clapping

Clap these patterns before playing:

Pattern A: | ♩ ♩ ♩ ♩ | (1 2 3 4) Pattern B: | 𝅗𝅥 ♩ ♩ | (1──2 3 4) Pattern C: | ♩ ♩ 𝅗𝅥 | (1 2 3──4) Pattern D: | ♪♪ ♩ ♪♪ ♩ | (1& 2 3& 4)

🎵 Waltz Time (3/4): My Bonnie

3/4 time — count: 1 2 3, 1 2 3 RH: | C - - | E - - | G - E | C - - | 1 2 3 1 2 3 1 2 3 1 2 3 LH: | C─────── | C─────── | C─────── | C─────── |

Feel the "OOM-pah-pah" of a waltz: strong beat 1, lighter beats 2 and 3.

Using a Metronome

A metronome keeps steady time. Start at:

  • 60 BPM for beginners (one tick per second)
  • Increase by 5 BPM when you can play perfectly
  • Target: 80-100 BPM for most beginner pieces

Free metronome apps: "Pro Metronome", "Soundbrenner", or Google "metronome".

Practice Task

  1. Clap all 4 rhythm patterns (A-D) with a metronome at 60 BPM
  2. Play rhythm exercise 2 on piano
  3. Play Twinkle Twinkle while counting "1, 2, 3, 4" out loud
  4. Try the waltz pattern — feel the difference from 4/4
