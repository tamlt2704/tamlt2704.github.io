Chapter 4: Reactions & Equations

Balancing Chemical Equations

Law of Conservation of Mass: atoms in = atoms out.

Example: Combustion of methane

Unbalanced: CH₄ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O Balanced: CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O Check: C:1=1 ✓ H:4=4 ✓ O:4=4 ✓

Types of Reactions

1. Synthesis (Combination)

A + B → AB 2Na + Cl₂ → 2NaCl

2. Decomposition

AB → A + B 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ (electrolysis)

3. Single Replacement

A + BC → AC + B Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu (more reactive metal displaces less reactive)

4. Double Replacement

AB + CD → AD + CB AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl↓ + NaNO₃

5. Combustion

Fuel + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O

Activity Series (Metals)

Most reactive: K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Cu > Ag > Au :Least reactive

A metal can displace any metal below it in the series.

Signs a Reaction Occurred

  • Gas produced (bubbles)
  • Precipitate forms (solid appears)
  • Color change
  • Temperature change
  • Light emitted

Balancing Practice

Example: Fe₂O₃ + CO → Fe + CO₂

Step 1: Fe: 2 on left → put 2Fe on right Step 2: O: 3+1=4 left, need 4 right → put... Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂ Check: Fe:2=2 ✓ O:3+3=6, 6 ✓ C:3=3 ✓

Practice Problems

P1. Balance: Al + O₂ → Al₂O₃

Solution: 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃ Check: Al:4=4 ✓ O:6=6 ✓

P2. Identify reaction type: 2KClO₃ → 2KCl + 3O₂

Solution: Decomposition (one compound breaks into simpler substances)

P3. Balance: C₂H₆ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O

Solution: 2C₂H₆ + 7O₂ → 4CO₂ + 6H₂O Check: C:4=4 ✓ H:12=12 ✓ O:14=14 ✓

P4. Will this reaction occur? Cu + ZnSO₄ → ?

Solution: No — Cu is below Zn in activity series, cannot displace it.
