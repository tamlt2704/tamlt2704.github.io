Practice Problems

P1. Which has larger atomic radius: Na or Cl?

Solution: Na — atomic radius decreases across a period (left to right)

P2. Which is more reactive: K or Na? Why?

Solution: K — further down Group 1, outer electron is farther from nucleus, easier to lose

P3. How many valence electrons does Selenium (Se, Group 16) have?

Solution: 6 valence electrons (Group 16 = 6 valence e⁻)

P4. Arrange in order of increasing ionization energy: Li, B, N, F

Solution: Li < B < N < F (increases across period)
