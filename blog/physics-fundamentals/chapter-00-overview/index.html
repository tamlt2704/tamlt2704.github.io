Physics Fundamentals - Course Overview

Welcome to Physics!

Physics is the study of how the universe works — from the smallest particles to the largest galaxies.

Course Structure

ChapterTopicKey Concepts
1Motion & KinematicsDisplacement, velocity, acceleration
2Forces & Newton's LawsForce, mass, inertia, action-reaction
3Energy & WorkKinetic/potential energy, conservation
4Waves & SoundFrequency, wavelength, resonance
5Electricity & MagnetismCharge, current, fields
6ThermodynamicsHeat, entropy, laws of thermodynamics
7Modern PhysicsRelativity, quantum mechanics intro

Prerequisites

  • Basic algebra (solving equations, graphing)
  • Trigonometry fundamentals (sin, cos, tan)
  • Scientific notation and unit conversions
