Chapter 3: Energy & Work

Work

W = F · d · cosθ
  • θ=0°: W=Fd (force along motion)
  • θ=90°: W=0 (perpendicular)
  • θ=180°: W=-Fd (opposing motion)

Unit: Joule (J) = N·m

Energy Types

Kinetic Energy: KE = ½mv²

Gravitational PE: PE = mgh

Elastic PE (Spring): PE = ½kx²

Work-Energy Theorem

W_net = ΔKE = ½mv_f² - ½mv_i²

Conservation of Energy

KE_i + PE_i = KE_f + PE_f ½mv_i² + mgh_i = ½mv_f² + mgh_f Energy Bar Chart (Pendulum): Top Middle Bottom ┌───┐ ┌───┐ ┌───┐ │PE█│ │PE▄│ │ │ │ │ │KE▄│ │KE█│ └───┘ └───┘ └───┘ Total energy constant throughout

Power

P = W/t = Fv Unit: Watt (W) = J/s 1 hp = 746 W

Example: Roller Coaster

500 kg cart from rest at h=40m. Speed at h=15m?

mgh_i = ½mv² + mgh_f v² = 2g(40-15) = 490 v = 22.1 m/s

Practice Problems

P1. 2 kg ball thrown up at 15 m/s. Max height?

Solution: ½mv² = mgh → h = v²/(2g) = 225/19.6 = 11.5 m

P2. 1200 kg car: 10 m/s → 30 m/s. Net work done?

Solution: W = ½(1200)(900-100) = 480,000 J = 480 kJ

P3. 60 kg person climbs 5 m stairs in 8 s. Power?

Solution: P = mgh/t = 60(9.8)(5)/8 = 367.5 W

P4. 0.5 kg block slides from h=3m down frictionless ramp onto rough floor (μ_k=0.4). How far on floor?

Solution: mgh = μ_k mg × d d = h/μ_k = 3/0.4 = 7.5 m
