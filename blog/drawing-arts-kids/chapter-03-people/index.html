Chapter 3: Drawing People 👤

Faces Are Easy When You Know the Secret!

A face fits inside an oval, and everything has a specific spot.

Face Proportions

╭─────────╮ │ │ ← top of head │ ─ ─ ─ ─│ ← EYES go in the MIDDLE │ │ │ ▽ │ ← nose halfway between eyes and chin │ ─── │ ← mouth halfway between nose and chin ╰─────────╯

Exercise 1: Draw a Face (Front View) 😊

Step 1: Draw an oval Step 2: Light line across the middle (eye line) Step 3: Draw eyes on that line (two ovals with circles inside) Step 4: Nose below (small triangle or curve) Step 5: Mouth below nose (curved line) Step 6: Add eyebrows, ears, hair!

Exercise 3: Stick Figure People 🏃

Start simple! Stick figures show ACTION.

Standing: Walking: Running: Jumping: O O O O | /| /|\ \|/ /|\ / | | | | | / \ / \ / \ / \ / \ / \

Add details to make them interesting:

O ← add hair --|-- ← add sleeves /|\ | ← add belt / \ ← add shoes

Exercise 4: Full Body (Simple) 🧍

Step 1: Oval head Step 2: Rectangle body Step 3: Rectangle arms and legs Step 4: Add hands (circles) and feet (ovals) Step 5: Add clothes and details!

○ ← head ┌─┴─┐ │ │ ← body (rectangle) │ │ └─┬─┘ ┌┴┐ ─┘ └─ ← legs

Exercise 5: Draw Your Family! 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Draw your family members! Make each person different:

  • Different heights (adults taller)
  • Different hair (long, short, curly, straight)
  • Different clothes
  • Different accessories (glasses, hat, etc.)
O O o o /|\ /|\ /|\ /|\ /|\ /|\ /|\ /|\ / \ / \ / \ / \ Dad Mom You Sibling (tall)(med) (shorter)

Pro Tips 🌟

  • Heads are NOT perfect circles — they're more like eggs
  • Eyes are in the MIDDLE of the head (most beginners put them too high!)
  • Hands are hard — it's okay to hide them behind the back at first
  • Practice expressions — they bring your characters to life!
