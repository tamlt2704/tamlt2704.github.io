Chapter 5: Landscapes & Scenes 🏞️

The Three Layers of a Landscape

Every landscape has:

  1. Foreground (close, big, detailed) — bottom of page
  2. Middle ground (medium size) — middle of page
  3. Background (far away, small, less detail) — top of page
┌─────────────────────────────┐ │ ☀️ ☁️ ☁️ │ ← SKY (background) │ │ │ ⛰️ ⛰️ │ ← MOUNTAINS (background) │ 🌲 🌲 🏠 🌲 🌲 │ ← TREES/HOUSE (middle ground) │ ═══════════════════════════ │ ← PATH (foreground) │ 🌸 🌿 🌸 🌿 🌸 │ ← FLOWERS (foreground) └─────────────────────────────┘

Rule: Things far away are SMALLER and HIGHER on the page!

Exercise 3: Nature Scene 🌄

Draw a complete scene with these elements:

  1. Draw a horizon line (where sky meets ground)
  2. Add mountains in the background (triangles/curves)
  3. Add trees in the middle ground
  4. Add a path or river in the foreground
  5. Add sky details (sun, clouds, birds)

  • A rocket ship
  • Maybe an alien! 👽

Landscape Tips 🌟

  • Overlap objects to show depth (tree in front of mountain)
  • Far away = lighter colors, close up = darker colors
  • Add texture: grass lines, bark patterns, cloud puffs
  • The sky takes up MORE space than you think (2/3 of the page!)
