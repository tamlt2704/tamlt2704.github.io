How to Hold Your Pencil

Hold it like you're writing, but a little further back from the tip. Don't squeeze too hard! Your hand should feel relaxed.

Light lines = press gently (for sketching) Dark lines = press harder (for final drawing)

Exercise 1: Shape Parade 🎪

Draw a row of each shape across your paper:

  1. 5 circles (try to make them round!)
  2. 5 squares (keep the sides even)
