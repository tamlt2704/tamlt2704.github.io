What is Subtracting? 🤔

Subtracting means taking some away!

You have some. Some go away. Now you have LESS!

See It With Emoji! 👀

🍪🍪🍪🍪🍪 eat 2 = 🍪🍪🍪

5 cookies - 2 cookies = 3 cookies!

🎈🎈🎈🎈 pop 1 = 🎈🎈🎈

4 balloons - 1 balloon = 3 balloons!

🐥🐥🐥🐥🐥🐥 fly away 3 = 🐥🐥🐥

6 birds - 3 birds = 3 birds!

Counting Back on Fingers 🖐️

Want to do 5 - 2?

  1. Hold up 5 fingers ✋
  2. Put DOWN 2 fingers (fold them in!)
  3. Count what's left: 1, 2, 3!
  4. 5 - 2 = 3! 🎉

The -1 Trick 🪄

Taking away 1 is EASY! It's just the number before!

  • 4 - 1 = 3 (one step back!)
  • 7 - 1 = 6
  • 10 - 1 = 9

Just say the number before! ✨

Zero Means Nothing Left! 0️⃣

🍭🍭🍭 eat 3 = ... nothing!

3 - 3 = 0!

Zero means ALL GONE! Empty! Nothing there!

And anything - 0 = the same thing! (You took nothing away!)

5 - 0 = 5 (still all there! 😄)

Who Has More? 🤷

The difference tells you!

You have 🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎 (5 apples) Your friend has 🍎🍎🍎 (3 apples)

Who has more? YOU! By how many? 5 - 3 = 2 more!

🎲 Game Time!

Cookie Monster Subtraction 🍪

Put 7 crackers on a plate. Close your eyes! A grown-up takes some away. Open your eyes! How many are LEFT? How many did Cookie Monster eat?

Bowling 🎳

Set up 10 cups. Roll a ball! How many fell? How many are still standing?

10 - (fell down) = still standing!

Countdown Jump 🦘

Stand up! Start at 5. Jump and count back: 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, SIT DOWN! 😂

You did it! 🌟

You can take away! You're so smart! 🧠✨

High five! ✋

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Tip

Subtraction is harder than addition for most kids — that's normal! Use real objects they can physically remove (take away crackers, hide toys). "How many are left?" is the key question. Connect subtraction to daily life: "You had 4 strawberries, you ate 1, how many now?" Keep it playful! 🌈
