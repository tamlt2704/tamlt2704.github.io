These tricks will make your friends say "WOW!" 🤩

Trick 1: The 9s Finger Trick! 🖐️🖐️

Want to multiply by 9? Use your fingers!

Hold up all 10 fingers. To do 9 x 3:

Put DOWN finger number 3 (from the left).

What do you see?

  • 2 fingers on the LEFT
  • 7 fingers on the RIGHT
  • Answer: 27! 🎉

Try 9 x 4: put down finger 4!

  • 3 on the left, 6 on the right = 36!

MAGIC! 🪄

Trick 2: Doubles +1 🎯

If you know your doubles, you can do doubles +1!

  • 3 + 3 = 6, so... 3 + 4 = 7! (just one more!)
  • 4 + 4 = 8, so... 4 + 5 = 9!
  • 5 + 5 = 10, so... 5 + 6 = 11!

The secret: one number is just ONE bigger than the other! ✨

Trick 3: Making 10! 🎯🎯

These pairs ALWAYS make 10:

  • 8 + 2 = 10 ✌️
  • 7 + 3 = 10 🤟
  • 6 + 4 = 10 🖖
  • 5 + 5 = 10 ✋
  • 9 + 1 = 10 ☝️

Know these by heart and math gets SO much easier! 💪

Trick 4: Count On From the Bigger Number! 🔢

Adding 2 + 7? Don't start from 2!

Start from 7 (the bigger one!) and count up: 8, 9!

2 + 7 = 9!

Adding 1 + 8? Start from 8! Count: 9!

1 + 8 = 9!

Always start from the BIG number! It's faster! 🏎️

Trick 5: The Turnaround Trick! 🔄

Here's a secret: you can FLIP addition around!

2 + 5 = 5 + 2 (same answer: 7!)

3 + 8 = 8 + 3 (same answer: 11!)

So if 2 + 5 is hard, just think 5 + 2! Start from 5 and count 2 more! Easy! 🎉

Wow Your Friends! 🤩

Try this at school or with family:

  1. Ask someone to pick a number from 1 to 9
  2. Tell them to multiply it by 9 (or use the finger trick!)
  3. Ask them to add the two digits of their answer together
  4. The answer is ALWAYS 9! 🤯

(Example: 9 x 7 = 63, and 6 + 3 = 9! Magic!)
