What is Adding? 🤔

Adding means putting things together!

You have some. You get MORE. Now you have a BIGGER group!

See It With Emoji! 👀

🐱🐱 + 🐱 = 🐱🐱🐱

2 cats + 1 cat = 3 cats!

🍭🍭🍭 + 🍭🍭 = 🍭🍭🍭🍭🍭

3 lollipops + 2 lollipops = 5 lollipops!

🌟 + 🌟🌟🌟 = 🌟🌟🌟🌟

1 star + 3 stars = 4 stars!

The +1 Trick 🪄

Adding 1 is EASY! It's just the next number!

  • 3 + 1 = 4 (just count one more!)
  • 5 + 1 = 6
  • 9 + 1 = 10

See? Just say the next number! Magic! ✨

Doubles are Fun! 👯

Same + Same = Double!

  • 1 + 1 = 2 ✌️
  • 2 + 2 = 4 🍪🍪🍪🍪
  • 3 + 3 = 6 🎲🎲🎲🎲🎲🎲
  • 4 + 4 = 8 🐙 (like an octopus's legs!)
  • 5 + 5 = 10 ✋✋ (your two hands!)

Adding With Fingers ✋

Want to add 3 + 4?

  1. Hold up 3 fingers on one hand
  2. Hold up 4 fingers on the other hand
  3. Count ALL the fingers: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7!
  4. 3 + 4 = 7! 🎉

🎲 Game Time!

Dice Adding 🎲🎲

Roll two dice. Count the dots on each. Put them together! What's the total?

Domino Adding

Look at a domino. Add the two sides together!

Snack Adding 🍇

Put 2 grapes on one side. Put 3 on the other. Push them together. Count! Eat! 😋

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Tip

Use physical objects for adding — blocks, snacks, toys. Let your child physically push two groups together and count the total. The "counting on" strategy (start from the bigger number and count up) develops naturally — don't rush it. Doubles facts are often the first ones memorized, so practice those! 🌈
