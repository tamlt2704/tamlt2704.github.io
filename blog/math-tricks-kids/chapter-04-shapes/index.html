Let's Meet the Shapes! 👋

Here are your shape friends:

  • ⭕ Circle — round and round, NO corners!
  • ⬜ Square — 4 sides, ALL the same size!
  • 🔺 Triangle — 3 sides, like a mountain!
  • 🟫 Rectangle — 4 sides, 2 long and 2 short!
  • ⭐ Star — pointy and sparkly!
  • 💚 Heart — love shape!
  • 🔷 Diamond — a square standing on its tip!
  • 🥚 Oval — a stretched circle, like an egg!

How Many Sides? 🧐

Count the sides!

  • Circle: 0 sides (it's all curvy!)
  • Triangle: 3 sides 🔺
  • Square: 4 sides ⬜
  • Rectangle: 4 sides 🟫
  • Star: 5 points! ⭐

🔍 Shape Hunt!

Be a Shape Detective! Walk around your house. Can you find:

  • Something shaped like a circle? (plate, clock, coin) ⭕
  • Something shaped like a square? (window, cracker, block) ⬜
  • Something shaped like a triangle? (pizza slice, roof, hanger) 🔺
  • Something shaped like a rectangle? (door, book, phone) 🟫

How many did you find? Write it down! 📝

Shape Patterns 🔄

What comes next?

⭕ ⬜ ⭕ ⬜ ⭕ ???

Answer: ⬜! (circle, square, circle, square...)

🔺 🔺 ⭕ 🔺 🔺 ???

Answer: ⭕!

3D Shapes! 🎉

These shapes are NOT flat — you can hold them!

  • 🏀 Sphere — like a ball! Round everywhere!
  • 📦 Cube — like a box! 6 flat faces!
  • 🍦 Cone — like an ice cream cone! Pointy top!
  • 🥫 Cylinder — like a can! Round top and bottom!

🎨 Shape Art!

Make a picture using ONLY shapes!

  • 🏠 House = square + triangle on top!
  • 🚗 Car = rectangle + 2 circles (wheels!)
  • 🤖 Robot = squares and rectangles!
  • 🌸 Flower = circle + ovals for petals!

Grab paper and crayons and try it! 🖍️

🎲 Game Time!

Shape Bingo 🎯

Draw shapes on paper. Call out a shape name. Find it and mark it!

Shape Sorting 🗂️

Get blocks or cut-out shapes. Sort them: all circles here, all squares there!

Body Shapes 🤸

Make shapes with your body! Can you make a star? A circle with a friend? Stand like a triangle!

You did it! 🌟

You're a Shape Detective now! 🔍🔷

High five! ✋

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Tip

Point out shapes everywhere you go — road signs, food, buildings, nature. Let your child trace shapes with their finger. Cookie cutters, building blocks, and tangram puzzles are great hands-on tools. The key insight at this age: shapes are defined by their properties (sides, corners), not just how they look! 🌈
