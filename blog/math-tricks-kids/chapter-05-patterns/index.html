What is a Pattern? 🤔

A pattern is something that repeats! Over and over!

Like a song that goes: clap, stomp, clap, stomp, clap, stomp! 👏🦶👏🦶👏🦶

AB Patterns (Two Things!) 🔴🔵

The same two things, over and over:

🔴🔵🔴🔵🔴🔵 — red, blue, red, blue!

🐱🐶🐱🐶🐱🐶 — cat, dog, cat, dog!

👏🦶👏🦶👏🦶 — clap, stomp, clap, stomp!

What comes next?

🔴🔵🔴🔵🔴 ???

Answer: 🔵! You got it! 🎉

ABC Patterns (Three Things!) 🔴🔵🟡

Now with THREE things repeating:

🔴🔵🟡🔴🔵🟡🔴🔵🟡

Red, blue, yellow, red, blue, yellow!

🍎🍌🍇🍎🍌🍇 — apple, banana, grape!

What comes next?

🔴🔵🟡🔴🔵 ???

Answer: 🟡! Amazing! ⭐

Growing Patterns 📈

These patterns get BIGGER each time!

1, 2, 3, 4, ??? — 5!

🐛, 🐛🐛, 🐛🐛🐛, ??? — 🐛🐛🐛🐛!

Small, medium, big, ??? — BIGGER!

⭐, ⭐⭐, ⭐⭐⭐, ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — one more each time!

Make Your Own Patterns! 🎨

Use anything!

  • Toys: car, doll, car, doll 🚗🧸🚗🧸
  • Colors: red crayon, blue crayon, red, blue 🖍️
  • Sounds: loud, quiet, loud, quiet 📢🤫📢🤫
  • Body: jump, spin, jump, spin 🦘🌀🦘🌀
  • Food: grape, cracker, grape, cracker 🍇🍘

🎲 Game Time!

Pattern Train 🚂

Line up toys in a pattern. Ask a grown-up: "What comes next?" Now THEY guess!

Bead Patterns 📿

String beads in a pattern. Red, blue, red, blue! Make a bracelet!

Nature Patterns 🌿

Go outside! Find patterns: stripes on a leaf, petals on a flower, spots on a bug!

You did it! 🌟

High five! ✋

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Tip

Patterns are the foundation of algebraic thinking! Point out patterns everywhere: floor tiles, music, daily routines (wake up, eat, play, sleep — that's a pattern!). Let your child create patterns and describe them in their own words. Start with AB patterns and only move to ABC when they're confident. 🌈
