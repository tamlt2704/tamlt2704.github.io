next: Basics

tmux lets you create persistent terminal sessions that survive disconnections, split your terminal into multiple panes, and manage complex workflows from a single window.

Why tmux?

  • Persistent sessions — detach and reattach without losing state
  • SSH survival — connection drops won't kill your processes
  • Split panes — editor, server, logs side by side
  • Scriptable — automate complex layouts
  • Lightweight — runs everywhere with minimal resources
