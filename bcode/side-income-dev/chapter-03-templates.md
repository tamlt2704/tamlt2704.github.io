## The Strategy

You already have valuable knowledge sitting in your repos, your Notion workspace, and your head. Templates and digital products let you package that knowledge once and sell it forever. No servers to maintain, no support tickets at 2am, no churn.

**What you can sell:**
- **Starter kits/boilerplates:** Next.js SaaS template, React Native app starter, Django + Stripe setup
- **Notion templates:** Sprint planning, job search tracker, freelance CRM
- **Figma kits:** Dashboard UI kit, landing page components, email templates
- **Code snippets/utilities:** Auth system, payment integration, email templates
- **Checklists/playbooks:** Launch checklist, security audit guide, migration playbook

**Why this works for busy parents:**
- Build once, sell forever (zero marginal cost)
- No ongoing maintenance (update quarterly at most)
- No customer support emergencies
- Revenue while you're at the playground

## The Math

**Pricing psychology:**
| Product Type | Price Range | Why |
|-------------|-------------|-----|
| Notion template | $19-49 | Saves hours of setup |
| Starter kit (basic) | $49-99 | Saves days of boilerplate |
| Starter kit (premium) | $99-199 | Saves weeks of architecture decisions |
| Course/guide | $29-99 | Saves months of trial and error |

**Revenue scenarios:**
- Budget template ($29) × 10 sales/month = $290/mo
- SaaS starter kit ($99) × 20 sales/month = $1,980/mo
- Premium boilerplate ($149) × 15 sales/month = $2,235/mo

**The "before and after" marketing:**
- Before: "Spend 40 hours setting up auth, payments, email, and deployment"
- After: "Start building your actual product in 10 minutes"

## The Strategy: Platforms & Distribution

**Where to sell:**
- **Gumroad:** Simple, 10% fee, built-in audience discovery
- **Lemon Squeezy:** Modern alternative, handles EU VAT, 5% + 50¢
- **Your own site:** Stripe Checkout + download link (0% platform fee, but no discovery)

**Marketing channels that work:**
- Twitter/X: Build in public, show progress, share tips from your template
- Dev.to / Hashnode: Write tutorials that naturally lead to your product
- YouTube: "Build X in 10 minutes with my template" videos
- Product Hunt: Good for launch day spike
- SEO: "[framework] starter kit" and "[framework] boilerplate" get searched constantly

## Action Items

- [ ] Audit your existing projects — what setup have you done that others would pay to skip?
- [ ] Pick ONE template/kit idea that leverages your strongest skill
- [ ] Spend 2-3 sessions (6-8 hours) packaging it properly (clean code, README, docs)
- [ ] Create a Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy listing with screenshots and a demo
- [ ] Write one blog post or tweet thread showing the "before/after"
- [ ] Price it at $49+ (you can always discount later, but you can't easily raise prices)

## Time Required

**Creating the product:** 8-15 hours (over 2-3 weeks)
**Setting up the store:** 1-2 hours
**Marketing (ongoing):** 1-2 hours/week (one post or tutorial)
**Maintenance:** 1-2 hours/month (updates, responding to questions)

## Real Examples

- **Shipfast:** A Next.js boilerplate for launching SaaS apps. Priced at $199. Creator reports $45K+ in revenue. One developer, built over a few weeks.
- **Gravity:** A SaaS starter kit. Solo dev, earns $2-4K/month consistently from a product built once.
- **Notion template creators:** Multiple creators earn $2-10K/month selling planning templates, CRM systems, and productivity dashboards.
- **Tailwind UI:** While this is a team effort now, it started as one developer's component collection. Earned $2M+ in the first year.

## Pitfalls

- **Pitfall #1: Giving it away free "for exposure."** Exposure doesn't pay rent. Charge from day one.
- **Pitfall #2: Over-engineering the template.** Ship a clean, working version. Add features based on customer requests.
- **Pitfall #3: No documentation.** A template without a README is a support nightmare. Spend 20% of your time on docs.
- **Pitfall #4: Pricing too low.** A $9 template signals "low quality." A $79 template signals "this will save me serious time."
- **Pitfall #5: Not showing the value visually.** Screenshots, GIFs, and demo videos sell templates. A wall of text doesn't.
