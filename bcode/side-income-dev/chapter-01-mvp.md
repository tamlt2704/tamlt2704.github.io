## The Strategy

Your first side income product doesn't need code. It needs a landing page, a price, and a Stripe link. The goal isn't to build something perfect — it's to answer one question: **"Will anyone pay for this?"**

Most developers fail because they build for months in isolation. Instead, sell first, build second. A landing page that describes your solution + a "Buy Now" button tells you everything you need to know in 48 hours.

**The "Will Anyone Pay?" Test:**
1. Describe the problem in one sentence
2. Describe your solution in one sentence
3. Put a price on it
4. Share it where your target audience hangs out
5. If someone clicks "Buy" — congratulations, you have a business

**The Mom Test (adapted):**
- Don't ask "Would you use this?" (everyone says yes)
- Ask "What do you currently do to solve this?" (reveals real pain)
- Ask "How much time/money does that cost you?" (reveals willingness to pay)
- Ask "When did you last search for a solution?" (reveals urgency)

## The Math

| Approach | Time to First Dollar | Risk |
|----------|---------------------|------|
| Build for 3 months, then launch | 90+ days | High (might be wrong) |
| Landing page + Stripe link | 1-7 days | Low (fast feedback) |
| Pre-sell to 5 people | 1-3 days | Lowest (validated demand) |

**Cost of validation:** $0-12 (domain + Carrd or free Vercel deploy)
**Cost of building without validation:** 100+ hours of your life

## The Strategy: One-Hour MVP

**Minute 0-15:** Pick one problem you've solved at work. Write it as: "I help [who] do [what] without [pain point]."

**Minute 15-35:** Create a landing page (Carrd, Typedream, or a single HTML file on Vercel):
- Headline: the problem
- 3 bullet points: what they get
- Price
- Stripe payment link (takes 5 minutes to set up)

**Minute 35-50:** Write 3 posts for Reddit/Twitter/HN where your audience lives. Not spammy — genuinely helpful, with a link to your page.

**Minute 50-60:** Set up a simple analytics tracker (Plausible, or just check Stripe dashboard). Walk away.

## Action Items

- [ ] Write down the ONE problem you've solved at work that others struggle with
- [ ] Create a Stripe account (if you don't have one)
- [ ] Build a one-page landing page describing your solution
- [ ] Set a price (start at $29-49 for a tool, $19-29 for a template)
- [ ] Share in 2-3 places where your target audience exists
- [ ] Wait 48 hours. If zero interest, pick a different problem. If clicks but no buys, adjust pricing or messaging.

## Time Required

**Initial MVP:** 1 hour (seriously)
**Validation period:** 48-72 hours of waiting
**Iteration:** 1-2 hours adjusting based on feedback

## Real Examples

- **Screenshot tool:** Dev noticed his team spent hours setting up Puppeteer for social images. Landing page: "Generate social images via API. $29/mo." First customer within a week.
- **Tailwind component pack:** Dev had a personal component library. Put 20 components on a Gumroad page for $39. Sold 5 copies the first weekend from one tweet.
- **Database migration guide:** Dev wrote a Notion doc about migrating from MongoDB to Postgres. Sold it for $19 on Gumroad. Made $400 in the first month from a single Reddit post.

## Pitfalls

- **Pitfall #1: "Let me just add one more feature first."** No. Ship the ugly version.
- **Pitfall #2: Asking friends for validation.** Friends lie to be nice. Strangers with credit cards don't.
- **Pitfall #3: Pricing too low.** $5 attracts tire-kickers. $29+ attracts people with real problems.
- **Pitfall #4: Sharing in the wrong places.** Developer tools → HN, Reddit, Twitter. Business tools → LinkedIn, niche forums.
- **Pitfall #5: Giving up after one failed attempt.** Most successful indie devs tried 3-5 ideas before one stuck.
