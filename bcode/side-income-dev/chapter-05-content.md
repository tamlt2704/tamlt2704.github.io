## The Strategy

You already learn things at work. You already solve problems. The "learn in public" flywheel means writing about what you learn — and getting paid for it. Technical writing is the lowest-risk side income because you're monetizing knowledge you already have.

**Content income streams:**
- **Paid newsletters:** Subscribers pay $5-10/month for your expertise
- **Sponsored blog posts:** Companies pay $200-2000 for a post reaching your audience
- **Course platforms:** Package your knowledge into video/text courses
- **Technical writing for companies:** Write docs or tutorials for $200-500/article
- **Affiliate content:** Honest reviews of tools you actually use

**The "Learn in Public" Flywheel:**
1. Learn something at work
2. Write about it (blog post, newsletter, tweet thread)
3. Build an audience of people who trust your expertise
4. Monetize through sponsorships, paid content, or products
5. Repeat — each piece compounds your authority

## The Math

**Paid newsletter math:**
- 500 free subscribers → 50 paid ($8/mo) = $400/mo
- 2,000 free subscribers → 200 paid ($8/mo) = $1,600/mo
- 5,000 free subscribers → 400 paid ($8/mo) = $3,200/mo

**Sponsored content:**
- 1,000 newsletter subscribers: $100-300 per sponsor
- 5,000 subscribers: $300-800 per sponsor
- 10,000+ subscribers: $800-2,000 per sponsor

**Course income:**
- Udemy course (passive): $200-2,000/mo depending on topic and reviews
- Self-hosted course ($99-299): 10 sales/month = $990-2,990/mo
- Cohort-based (premium): $500-2000/student, 10 students = $5,000-20,000 per cohort

**Time investment:** 2-3 hours/week writing. That's one early morning session and one lunch break.

## The Strategy: Platforms & Approach

**Newsletter platforms:**
- **Substack:** Free to start, takes 10% of paid subscriptions. Built-in discovery.
- **Buttondown:** Developer-friendly, cheaper (starts free, $9/mo for paid features). More control.
- **ConvertKit:** More features, better for selling courses alongside newsletter.

**Course platforms:**
- **Udemy:** Massive audience, but low prices ($10-15 per sale) and you don't own the relationship
- **Teachable/Podia:** You set the price, you own the audience. Better margins.
- **Gumroad:** Simple for text-based courses or guides

**Blog monetization:**
- **Hashnode/Dev.to:** Free hosting, built-in audience for discovery
- **Your own blog:** Full control, better for SEO long-term
- **Carbon Ads / EthicalAds:** Developer-focused ad networks ($1-5 CPM)

## Action Items

- [ ] Pick ONE topic you know well enough to write about weekly
- [ ] Start a free newsletter (Substack or Buttondown) — commit to 4 posts before evaluating
- [ ] Write your first post about something you learned this week at work
- [ ] Share each post in 2-3 relevant communities
- [ ] After 10 posts and 200+ subscribers, consider adding a paid tier
- [ ] Reach out to one developer tool company about sponsored content

## Time Required

**Writing one post:** 1.5-2.5 hours
**Weekly commitment:** 2-3 hours (one post + promotion)
**Course creation:** 20-40 hours total (spread over 1-2 months)
**Ongoing:** Consistent 2-3 hours/week indefinitely

## Real Examples

- **ByteByteGo:** System design newsletter. Started as a side project, now earns $500K+/year. One person writing about what they know.
- **TLDR Newsletter:** Tech news digest. Started as a solo side project, grew to millions of subscribers.
- **Josh Comeau:** Blog about CSS/React. Turned into a course business earning $1M+/year. Started with free blog posts.
- **Paid technical writers:** Many developers earn $200-500/article writing for companies like DigitalOcean, LogRocket, Twilio, and Auth0.

## Pitfalls

- **Pitfall #1: Waiting until you're an "expert."** You only need to be one step ahead of your reader. Write about what you learned today.
- **Pitfall #2: Inconsistency.** One post per week for 6 months beats 10 posts in one week then silence.
- **Pitfall #3: Writing for other developers only.** The biggest audiences are beginners. Don't gatekeep with jargon.
- **Pitfall #4: Monetizing too early.** Build trust with 10-20 free posts before asking for money.
- **Pitfall #5: Perfectionism.** A "good enough" post published today beats a perfect post published never. Ship it.
