# Side Income for Developers — Ship Products in Stolen Hours

A practical course for developers with families who want to earn extra income from code — but only have 5-10 hours per week. No "quit your job and hustle" nonsense. Real strategies that work in the margins of a busy life.

## Prerequisites

- A day job (this is supplemental income, not a replacement)
- 5-10 hours per week (naptime, early mornings, lunch breaks)
- Existing dev skills (we don't teach coding here — we teach shipping)

## Philosophy

You don't need a startup. You don't need VC funding. You don't need to sacrifice family time. You need ONE small product that solves ONE real problem for people willing to pay. Ship small, ship fast, iterate based on revenue — not vanity metrics.
