# Home Automation for Developers — Your House, Your Rules

A practical course on home automation for developers who want their house to be smarter without paying monthly subscriptions or trusting cloud services. Everything runs locally. Everything is hackable.

## Prerequisites

- A Raspberry Pi 4+ or old laptop/NUC
- Basic networking knowledge (IP addresses, WiFi)
- $50-100 for initial hardware (Zigbee stick + a few sensors)

## Philosophy

Home automation should make life easier for the WHOLE family — not just the developer. If your partner can't use it, it's a toy, not automation. If it breaks when the internet goes down, it's a liability. Local-first, family-friendly, reliable.
