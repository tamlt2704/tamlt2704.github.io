## The Quest

You emerge from the Underground Kingdom into the **Wizard's Arena**. Two wizards are having a spell-casting duel. Each wizard's power grows over time, but at different rates. The question: at what moment will they have EQUAL power?

> "This is where it gets interesting," says Professor Zap, munching popcorn. "When BOTH sides of the equation have x in them, you need to collect all the x's onto one side. I call it: herding the variables!"

## The New Challenge

Until now, x was only on ONE side:
- `x + 5 = 12` ← x is only on the left

But what about this?
- `3x + 2 = x + 10` ← x is on BOTH sides!

You can't just subtract a number to isolate x — there are x's everywhere! You need a new strategy.

## Strategy: Get All X's on One Side

**Problem:** 3x + 2 = x + 10

**Step 1:** Move all x's to one side. Subtract x from both sides:
```
    3x + 2 = x + 10
    3x - x + 2 = x - x + 10    ← subtract x from both sides
    2x + 2 = 10                  ← the x's on the right disappear!
```

**Step 2:** Now it's a regular two-step equation! Subtract 2:
```
    2x + 2 = 10
    2x = 8                       ← subtract 2 from both sides
```

**Step 3:** Divide by 2:
```
    2x = 8
    x = 4 ✓
```

**Check:** Plug x = 4 into BOTH sides of the original:
- Left side: 3(4) + 2 = 12 + 2 = 14
- Right side: 4 + 10 = 14
- 14 = 14 ✓ They match!

## The Process

```
    ┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  1. Move all x's to one side        │
    │  2. Move all numbers to the other   │
    │  3. Solve the simple equation       │
    │  4. CHECK your answer!              │
    └─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> "Think of it like sorting laundry," says Professor Zap. "Variables go in one basket, numbers go in the other. Once they're sorted, the answer is easy!"

## More Examples

**Example 1:** 5x = 3x + 12
```
    5x = 3x + 12
    5x - 3x = 12          ← subtract 3x from both sides
    2x = 12
    x = 6 ✓

    Check: 5(6) = 30, and 3(6) + 12 = 18 + 12 = 30 ✓
```

**Example 2:** 4x + 3 = 2x + 11
```
    4x + 3 = 2x + 11
    4x - 2x + 3 = 11      ← subtract 2x from both sides
    2x + 3 = 11
    2x = 8                 ← subtract 3 from both sides
    x = 4 ✓

    Check: 4(4) + 3 = 19, and 2(4) + 11 = 19 ✓
```

**Example 3:** 6x - 5 = 4x + 7
```
    6x - 5 = 4x + 7
    2x - 5 = 7            ← subtract 4x from both sides
    2x = 12                ← add 5 to both sides
    x = 6 ✓

    Check: 6(6) - 5 = 31, and 4(6) + 7 = 31 ✓
```

## Which Side Do You Move X To?

It doesn't matter! You'll get the same answer either way. But here's a tip:

> "Move x to the side where it has the BIGGER number in front," Professor Zap suggests. "That way you avoid negative numbers. Less confusing!"

**Example:** 2x + 5 = 8x - 7

The 8x is bigger than 2x, so move x's to the RIGHT:
```
    2x + 5 = 8x - 7
    5 = 8x - 2x - 7       ← subtract 2x from both sides
    5 = 6x - 7
    12 = 6x                ← add 7 to both sides
    2 = x ✓
```

## The Importance of Checking

Always plug your answer back into the ORIGINAL equation. Both sides should give the same number.

If they don't match, you made an error somewhere. Go back and try again. Mistakes aren't failures — they're clues!

## The Wizard Duel Quest ⚡

Two wizards are casting spells. Their power levels grow each round:

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  WIZARD DUEL                                 ║
║                                              ║
║  Wizard Aria's power: 4x + 5                ║
║  (starts at 5, gains 4 per round)            ║
║                                              ║
║  Wizard Bolt's power: 2x + 17               ║
║  (starts at 17, gains 2 per round)           ║
║                                              ║
║  x = the round number                        ║
║                                              ║
║  Question: On which round will they have     ║
║  EQUAL power?                                ║
║                                              ║
║  BONUS: What is their power level at that    ║
║  moment?                                     ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

Set their powers equal:
- 4x + 5 = 2x + 17
- 4x - 2x + 5 = 17
- 2x + 5 = 17
- 2x = 12
- x = **6**

They have equal power on **round 6**!

BONUS: Power at round 6:
- Aria: 4(6) + 5 = 24 + 5 = **29**
- Bolt: 2(6) + 17 = 12 + 17 = **29** ✓

Both wizards hit power level 29 at the same moment. After that, Aria pulls ahead (she gains power faster). Before that, Bolt was winning (he started higher).

</details>

## Level Up! 📈

What you learned:
- When x appears on BOTH sides, move all x's to one side
- Move numbers to the other side
- Then solve like a regular equation
- **Always check** by plugging your answer into both sides
- It doesn't matter which side you move x to — pick the easier one

## Did You Know? 🌟

Equations with variables on both sides are used in business all the time! If Company A charges $5 per month plus $2 per item, and Company B charges $17 per month plus $0 per item, you can figure out exactly how many items make them cost the same. It's the same math you just did with the wizards!
