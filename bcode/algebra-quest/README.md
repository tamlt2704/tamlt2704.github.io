# Algebra Quest: The Adventure Begins

Welcome to **Numeria** — a fantasy world where every door, treasure chest, and dragon puzzle runs on algebra. You're a young adventurer who just discovered a mysterious scroll covered in symbols. Your guide? **Professor Zap**, a quirky wizard who believes every problem in the universe can be solved with the right equation.

This isn't a textbook. It's a quest. You'll solve puzzles, defeat monsters, and unlock treasures — all with real algebra.

## Who Is This For?

- Ages 10–14 (or anyone who wants to learn algebra from scratch)
- No prior algebra needed — just basic arithmetic (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division)
- You should be comfortable with numbers up to about 100
- Curiosity required. Pencil recommended. Calculator optional.

## The Story

You're exploring the kingdom of Numeria when you stumble upon an ancient scroll. The moment you touch it, **Professor Zap** appears in a puff of purple smoke.

> "Ah, a new adventurer! That scroll you're holding? It's written in the language of algebra. Every puzzle in this kingdom — every locked door, every sleeping dragon, every hidden treasure — can be solved if you learn to read it. Shall I teach you?"

Your quest begins.

## How to Use This Course

1. **Read the story** — each chapter starts with a quest
2. **Learn the concept** — Professor Zap explains with pictures and examples
3. **Try the practice problems** — answers are included so you can check
4. **Complete the quest** — a story problem that uses everything you learned
5. **Level up!** — review what you mastered

## Tips for Success

- **Write things down.** Algebra is easier when you can see your work on paper.
- **Mistakes are treasure.** Every wrong answer teaches you something. Professor Zap makes mistakes too (he'll never admit it).
- **Go at your own pace.** There's no timer. No score. Just learning.
- **If you're stuck, re-read the examples.** Then try again. Then try a different way.
- **Have fun.** Seriously. Algebra is a puzzle game. Treat it like one.

## What You'll Be Able to Do

By the end of this quest, you'll be able to:

- Understand what variables are and how to use them
- Solve one-step and two-step equations
- Work with negative numbers
- Solve equations with variables on both sides
- Understand and solve inequalities
- Recognize patterns and write function rules
- Translate word problems into equations
- Feel confident walking into your first algebra class
