## The Quest

You've made it. The final chamber. Before you stands the **Equation Dragon** — a massive creature whose scales shimmer with numbers and symbols. It speaks only in algebra, and the only way to defeat it is to solve every puzzle it throws at you.

Professor Zap stands beside you, staff raised:

> "This is it, adventurer. Everything you've learned — variables, balance, multiplication, two-step equations, negatives, both sides, inequalities, patterns, word problems — it ALL comes together here. You have the tools. You have the knowledge. Now show this dragon what you're made of!"

## Round 1: The Warm-Up Scales 🐉

The dragon breathes three equations at you. Solve them to crack its outer armor:

**1a.** x + 14 = 23

**1b.** 5x = 45

**1c.** x/3 = 7

<details>
<summary>Round 1 Answers</summary>

- 1a. x = 9 (subtract 14)
- 1b. x = 9 (divide by 5)
- 1c. x = 21 (multiply by 3)

The dragon's outer scales crack! "Lucky shots," it growls.

</details>

## Round 2: Two-Step Fire Blasts 🔥

The dragon attacks with harder equations. Two steps each:

**2a.** 3x + 7 = 22

**2b.** 4x - 9 = 15

**2c.** x/2 + 6 = 11

**2d.** 5x - 3 = 32

<details>
<summary>Round 2 Answers</summary>

- 2a. 3x = 15, x = **5**
- 2b. 4x = 24, x = **6**
- 2c. x/2 = 5, x = **10**
- 2d. 5x = 35, x = **7**

The dragon staggers! "You're stronger than I expected..."

</details>

## Round 3: The Negative Breath ❄️

The dragon switches to ice attacks — negative numbers!

**3a.** x + 8 = 3

**3b.** -2x = 14

**3c.** x - 5 = -12

**3d.** (-3) × (-4) + x = 7

<details>
<summary>Round 3 Answers</summary>

- 3a. x = 3 - 8 = **-5**
- 3b. x = 14 ÷ (-2) = **-7**
- 3c. x = -12 + 5 = **-7**
- 3d. 12 + x = 7, x = 7 - 12 = **-5**

The dragon's ice armor shatters! "Impossible! You understand the underground numbers!"

</details>

## Round 4: Variables on Both Sides ⚔️

The dragon splits into two forms — you must find where they're equal:

**4a.** 5x + 3 = 2x + 18

**4b.** 7x - 4 = 3x + 16

**4c.** 6x + 1 = 4x + 11

<details>
<summary>Round 4 Answers</summary>

- 4a. 3x + 3 = 18, 3x = 15, x = **5**
  Check: 5(5)+3 = 28, 2(5)+18 = 28 ✓
- 4b. 4x - 4 = 16, 4x = 20, x = **5**
  Check: 7(5)-4 = 31, 3(5)+16 = 31 ✓
- 4c. 2x + 1 = 11, 2x = 10, x = **5**
  Check: 6(5)+1 = 31, 4(5)+11 = 31 ✓

The two dragon forms merge back into one, weakened. "You can see through my illusions!"

</details>

## Round 5: The Inequality Shield 🛡️

The dragon raises a shield. You need to find the RANGE of values that breaks through:

**5a.** 2x + 1 > 9

**5b.** 3x - 6 ≤ 12

**5c.** -4x < 20

<details>
<summary>Round 5 Answers</summary>

- 5a. 2x > 8, x > **4**
- 5b. 3x ≤ 18, x ≤ **6**
- 5c. x > **-5** (divided by -4, flipped the sign!)

The shield cracks! Any value of x between 5 and 6 (inclusive) breaks through all three barriers at once.

</details>

## Round 6: The Pattern Prediction 🔮

The dragon's attacks follow a pattern. Predict the next one to dodge:

| Attack # | Damage |
|---|---|
| 1 | 7 |
| 2 | 11 |
| 3 | 15 |
| 4 | 19 |
| 5 | ? |

**6a.** What's the rule?
**6b.** What's the damage of attack #5?
**6c.** On which attack will the damage first exceed 40?

<details>
<summary>Round 6 Answers</summary>

- 6a. Damage goes up by 4 each time. At attack 1: 4(1) + 3 = 7 ✓
  Rule: **damage = 4n + 3** (where n = attack number)
- 6b. 4(5) + 3 = **23 damage**
- 6c. 4n + 3 > 40 → 4n > 37 → n > 9.25 → attack **#10**

You dodge just in time! The dragon is impressed.

</details>

## Round 7: The Word Problem Riddle 📜

The dragon speaks its final riddle:

> "I am the Equation Dragon. My age is a mystery. If you triple my age and subtract 15, you get the same as doubling my age and adding 20. How old am I?"

**Set up the equation and solve!**

<details>
<summary>Round 7 Answer</summary>

Let x = the dragon's age.

"Triple my age and subtract 15" = 3x - 15
"Doubling my age and adding 20" = 2x + 20

They're equal:
- 3x - 15 = 2x + 20
- 3x - 2x = 20 + 15
- x = **35**

The Equation Dragon is 35 years old!

Check: 3(35) - 15 = 90, and 2(35) + 20 = 90 ✓

</details>

## The Final Blow ⚡

The dragon collapses, then... laughs.

> "Well done, adventurer. You've proven yourself worthy. I wasn't really trying to stop you — I was TESTING you. And you passed."

The dragon's scales rearrange into a staircase, revealing a treasure room behind it.

> "Take what you've earned. You now speak the language of algebra. Every locked door, every puzzle, every challenge in Numeria — you have the tools to solve them all."
