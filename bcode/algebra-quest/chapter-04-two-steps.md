## The Quest

Deep in the Numeria Vault, you find a treasure chest with TWO locks on it. A sign reads:

```
╔══════════════════════════════════╗
║  This chest has two locks.       ║
║  You must undo them in the       ║
║  correct ORDER.                  ║
║                                  ║
║  Outer lock first. Inner lock    ║
║  second. Get it wrong and the    ║
║  chest resets.                   ║
╚══════════════════════════════════╝
```

> "Two-step equations!" Professor Zap rubs his hands together. "These are like presents wrapped in two layers of paper. You have to unwrap the outer layer first, THEN the inner layer. Do it in the wrong order and you'll just make a mess."

## The Idea: Unwrapping a Present

Think of the equation `2x + 3 = 11` as a present wrapped in two layers:

```
    Layer 2 (outer): + 3     ← wrapping paper
    Layer 1 (inner): × 2     ← the box
    The gift: x              ← the mystery number!
```

To unwrap:
1. **Remove the outer layer first** (undo the + 3)
2. **Then remove the inner layer** (undo the × 2)

## Solving 2x + 3 = 11

**Step 1:** Undo the outer layer (+3) by subtracting 3 from both sides:
```
    2x + 3 = 11
    2x + 3 - 3 = 11 - 3
    2x = 8
```

**Step 2:** Undo the inner layer (×2) by dividing both sides by 2:
```
    2x = 8
    2x ÷ 2 = 8 ÷ 2
    x = 4 ✓
```

**Check:** 2(4) + 3 = 8 + 3 = 11 ✓ 

## The Order Matters!

> "Remember," says Professor Zap, wagging his finger, "the order is:
>
> 1. Undo **addition or subtraction** FIRST (outer layer)
> 2. Undo **multiplication or division** SECOND (inner layer)
>
> It's the opposite of the order you'd use to BUILD the expression. You're un-building it!"

Think of getting dressed vs. getting undressed:
- Getting dressed: socks first, then shoes
- Getting undressed: shoes first, then socks

Same idea! Undo in reverse order.

## More Examples

**Example 1:** 3x - 5 = 16
```
    Step 1: Undo the -5 (add 5 to both sides)
    3x - 5 + 5 = 16 + 5
    3x = 21

    Step 2: Undo the ×3 (divide both sides by 3)
    3x ÷ 3 = 21 ÷ 3
    x = 7 ✓

    Check: 3(7) - 5 = 21 - 5 = 16 ✓
```

**Example 2:** 4x + 1 = 25
```
    Step 1: Undo the +1 (subtract 1 from both sides)
    4x + 1 - 1 = 25 - 1
    4x = 24

    Step 2: Undo the ×4 (divide both sides by 4)
    4x ÷ 4 = 24 ÷ 4
    x = 6 ✓

    Check: 4(6) + 1 = 24 + 1 = 25 ✓
```

**Example 3:** 5x - 10 = 0
```
    Step 1: Undo the -10 (add 10 to both sides)
    5x - 10 + 10 = 0 + 10
    5x = 10

    Step 2: Undo the ×5 (divide both sides by 5)
    5x ÷ 5 = 10 ÷ 5
    x = 2 ✓

    Check: 5(2) - 10 = 10 - 10 = 0 ✓
```

## Common Mistakes (and How to Avoid Them)

❌ **Wrong:** Dividing first before subtracting
```
    2x + 3 = 11
    x + 3 = 5.5    ← WRONG! You can't just divide the 2x
                       without dividing the +3 too!
```

✅ **Right:** Always undo addition/subtraction FIRST
```
    2x + 3 = 11
    2x = 8          ← subtract 3 from both sides first
    x = 4           ← THEN divide by 2
```

> "Think of it this way," says Professor Zap. "The +3 is the outer wrapping. You can't open the box until you remove the wrapping!"

## The Two-Lock Chest Quest 🔐

The chest in the vault has this inscription:

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  LOCK 1 (outer): Solve for x            ║
║     3x + 7 = 28                          ║
║                                          ║
║  LOCK 2 (inner): Solve for y            ║
║     2y - x = 8                           ║
║     (use your answer for x!)             ║
║                                          ║
║  The chest opens when you enter: x + y   ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

<details>
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Lock 1:**
- 3x + 7 = 28
- 3x = 21 (subtract 7)
- x = 7 (divide by 3)

**Lock 2:**
- 2y - x = 8, and x = 7
- 2y - 7 = 8
- 2y = 15 (add 7)
- y = 7.5 (divide by 2)

**The code:** x + y = 7 + 7.5 = **14.5**

The chest clicks open! Inside: a map to the Underground Kingdom... and a note that says "Bring a warm coat. It's cold down there." 🥶

</details>

## Level Up! 📈

What you learned:
- Two-step equations need TWO operations to solve
- **Order matters:** undo addition/subtraction FIRST, then multiplication/division
- Think of it as "unwrapping a present" — outer layer first
- Always check your answer by plugging it back into the original equation
- Answers can be decimals or fractions — that's totally fine!

## Did You Know? 🌟

Video game designers use two-step equations all the time! If a character's damage formula is `2 × attack_power + 5 = total_damage`, they can figure out what attack power is needed to defeat a boss with a certain amount of health. You're basically learning game design math right now.
