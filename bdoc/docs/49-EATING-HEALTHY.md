# Chapter 49: Eating Healthy — A Developer's Guide to Fuel That Works

- Nutrition fundamentals (macros, micros, calories — no fad diets)

## PART 1: How Food Works (The System)

## 49.1 Think of food like system resources

```
Your body is a machine. Food is the input.

BAD INPUT (junk food):
  → Energy spikes and crashes (like memory leaks)
  → Brain fog (like high CPU with no useful output)
  → Inflammation (like accumulated technical debt)
  → Weight gain (like unmanaged storage growth)

GOOD INPUT (real food):
  → Stable energy all day (like a well-tuned server)
  → Clear focus (like an optimized query)
  → Good recovery (like proper garbage collection)
  → Consistent performance (like a load-balanced system)
```

## 49.2 Macronutrients — the three building blocks

| Macro | What it does | Sources | Target |
|-------|-------------|---------|--------|
| **Protein** | Builds/repairs muscle, keeps you full, brain function | Chicken, fish, eggs, beans, tofu, Greek yogurt, lentils | 1.6–2.2g per kg bodyweight |
| **Carbohydrates** | Primary energy source (especially for brain) | Rice, oats, potatoes, fruit, bread, pasta | 40–50% of calories |
| **Fat** | Hormones, brain health, vitamin absorption, energy | Avocado, nuts, olive oil, fish, eggs | 25–35% of calories |

**Calories:** Energy unit. Your body burns a certain amount daily (TDEE):
```
Sedentary desk worker (70kg):  ~2000 kcal/day
Active (exercise 3-4x/week):  ~2400 kcal/day
Very active:                   ~2800 kcal/day

Lose weight:  eat 300–500 below TDEE
Maintain:     eat at TDEE
Gain muscle:  eat 200–300 above TDEE (with resistance training)
```

> **You don't need to count calories forever.** Count for 2 weeks to learn what portions look like. Then you can estimate by eye.

## 49.3 Micronutrients — the hidden performance boosters

| Nutrient | Why developers need it | Found in |
|----------|----------------------|----------|
| **Iron** | Oxygen to brain, prevents fatigue | Red meat, spinach, lentils, fortified cereal |
| **Omega-3** | Brain function, focus, mood | Salmon, sardines, walnuts, flax seeds |
| **Magnesium** | Sleep quality, stress management, muscle relaxation | Dark chocolate, almonds, avocado, bananas |
| **Vitamin D** | Mood, immunity (you're indoors all day!) | Sunlight, fatty fish, fortified milk, supplements |
| **B vitamins** | Energy metabolism, nerve function | Eggs, meat, whole grains, leafy greens |
| **Zinc** | Immune function, wound healing | Meat, pumpkin seeds, chickpeas |
| **Vitamin C** | Immunity, collagen, antioxidant | Citrus, bell peppers, broccoli, berries |

**If you do nothing else:** Take vitamin D (most indoor workers are deficient) and eat fatty fish twice a week (omega-3).

## PART 2: What To Eat (Simple Rules)

## 49.4 The plate model (no counting required)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                     │
│    ┌─────────┐  ┌─────────────┐    │
│    │         │  │             │    │
│    │ PROTEIN │  │ VEGETABLES  │    │
│    │  (1/4)  │  │   (1/2)    │    │
│    │         │  │             │    │
│    └─────────┘  └─────────────┘    │
│    ┌─────────────────────────┐      │
│    │      CARBS (1/4)        │      │
│    └─────────────────────────┘      │
│                                     │
│    + thumb of healthy fat           │
│    + glass of water                 │
│                                     │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘

Every meal: 1/2 vegetables, 1/4 protein, 1/4 carbs, a bit of fat.
That's it. No food scale needed.
```

## 49.5 The "good enough" food list

**Always good (eat freely):**
- Vegetables (any — raw, steamed, roasted, stir-fried)
- Fruits (whole — not juice)
- Lean protein (chicken breast, fish, eggs, tofu, legumes)
- Whole grains (brown rice, oats, quinoa, whole wheat bread)
- Healthy fats (avocado, nuts, olive oil, seeds)
- Water, tea, black coffee

**Moderate (eat in controlled portions):**
- White rice, pasta, bread (not evil — just less fibre)
- Cheese, full-fat dairy
- Red meat (2-3× per week is fine)
- Dark chocolate (>70% cocoa)
- Alcohol (1-2 drinks occasionally)

**Minimise (not "never" — just rare):**
- Sugary drinks (soda, fruit juice, sweetened coffee)
- Fried food (chips, deep-fried anything)
- Processed snacks (biscuits, crisps, candy)
- Ultra-processed meals (instant noodles daily, frozen pizza daily)
- Energy drinks (caffeine + sugar + chemicals)

> **The 80/20 rule:** Eat well 80% of the time. The other 20% — enjoy pizza, cake, whatever. Perfection isn't sustainable. Consistency beats intensity.

## 49.6 Developer-specific eating traps

| Trap | Problem | Fix |
|------|---------|-----|
| Skipping breakfast, huge dinner | Blood sugar crashes, poor focus in morning | Eat something within 1-2 hours of waking |
| Energy drinks for focus | Crash 2 hours later, sleep disruption, anxiety | Coffee (black or with milk) — max 2-3 cups, none after 2pm |
| Snacking while coding | Mindless calories, sugar spikes | Pre-portion snacks, keep them away from desk |
| Eating at desk | Overeating (no awareness), poor digestion | Take 15-min break, eat away from screen |
| Takeaway every night | High sodium, high fat, expensive | Meal prep Sundays (see Part 3) |
| "I'll eat later" (hyperfocus) | Skipping meals → binge later | Set a lunch alarm |
| Late-night coding + snacking | Weight gain, poor sleep | Kitchen closes after dinner |

## PART 3: Meal Prep (The Developer's Hack)

## 49.7 Why meal prep works for us

Meal prep is **batch processing for food**:
- Decide once (Sunday) instead of 21 times per week
- Cook once, eat 4-5 times (efficiency)
- Eliminates "what should I eat?" decision fatigue
- Cheaper than takeaway
- Healthier because you control ingredients

## 49.8 The system (2 hours on Sunday → food for the week)

```
STEP 1: Pick 2 proteins
  • Baked chicken thighs (season, bake 25 min at 200°C)
  • Hard-boiled eggs (boil 10 min, cool, peel)

STEP 2: Pick 2 carbs
  • Rice (rice cooker — zero effort)
  • Roasted sweet potatoes (chop, oil, salt, bake 30 min)

STEP 3: Pick 3 vegetables
  • Roasted broccoli + peppers (same oven as potatoes)
  • Raw salad (cucumber, tomato, carrot — chop and store separately)
  • Frozen stir-fry mix (buy pre-cut, cook fresh each meal in 5 min)

STEP 4: Assemble
  • 5 containers: protein + carb + veggie in each
  • Fridge: lasts 4-5 days
  • Add sauce/dressing fresh each day (prevents sogginess)
```

## 49.9 Zero-effort meals (when you can't even meal prep)

| Meal | Time | Effort |
|------|------|--------|
| Greek yogurt + banana + handful of nuts | 2 min | Assemble |
| Scrambled eggs (3) + toast + tomato | 5 min | Pan |
| Tin of tuna + pre-washed salad + olive oil | 3 min | Assemble |
| Overnight oats (oats + milk + fruit — made night before) | 2 min prep | Fridge does the work |
| Rotisserie chicken (pre-cooked from supermarket) + microwave rice + frozen veg | 5 min | Microwave |
| Bean and cheese quesadilla (tortilla + beans + cheese → pan 3 min each side) | 8 min | Pan |
| Peanut butter toast + apple | 2 min | Toaster |

## 49.10 Snacks that won't crash you

**Good (sustained energy):**
- Apple + peanut butter
- Handful of mixed nuts (almonds, walnuts, cashews)
- Greek yogurt + berries
- Carrot sticks + hummus
- Hard-boiled egg
- Dark chocolate (2-3 squares)
- Banana

**Bad (sugar spike → crash → tired):**
- Biscuits / cookies
- Crisps / chips
- Chocolate bars (Snickers, KitKat)
- Sugary granola bars
- Fruit juice
- Sweets / candy

## PART 4: Energy & Focus Through Food

## 49.11 The developer's ideal day

```
7:00  WAKE
      Glass of water (you're dehydrated from sleep)

7:30  BREAKFAST — steady energy
      Oats + banana + peanut butter + seeds
      OR eggs + toast + avocado
      Black coffee or tea

10:30 SNACK (optional — if hungry)
      Apple + handful of almonds
      OR Greek yogurt

12:30 LUNCH — the biggest meal
      Chicken/fish + rice + big portion of vegetables
      Water (not soda)
      AWAY from your desk. Walk for 5 min after.

15:30 SNACK — fight the afternoon slump
      Dark chocolate + nuts
      OR carrot + hummus
      Green tea (light caffeine without the crash)
      NO MORE COFFEE after this (sleep quality)

19:00 DINNER — lighter than lunch
      Fish/tofu + roasted vegetables + small portion carbs
      OR soup/stew + bread

21:00 KITCHEN CLOSED
      Herbal tea if you want something
      No screens 1 hour before bed (improves sleep → improves everything)
```

## 49.12 Foods for brain performance

| Goal | Eat | Avoid |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Sustained focus** | Complex carbs (oats, brown rice), protein, healthy fat | Simple sugar (candy, soda, white bread on its own) |
| **Memory** | Fatty fish (salmon, sardines), blueberries, walnuts | Excessive alcohol, trans fats |
| **Mood** | Dark leafy greens, nuts, dark chocolate, fermented foods | Ultra-processed food, excess sugar |
| **Sleep quality** | Magnesium (almonds, dark chocolate), tart cherries, chamomile tea | Caffeine after 2pm, heavy meals before bed, alcohol |
| **Reduce inflammation** | Turmeric, ginger, berries, olive oil, fatty fish | Fried food, processed meat, excess sugar |

## 49.13 Hydration

```
Most developers are mildly dehydrated → headaches, poor focus, fatigue

Target: 2–3 litres per day (8–12 glasses)

Tricks:
• Keep a water bottle at your desk (visible = reminder)
• Drink a glass on every break (stand up → water)
• Tea counts. Coffee counts (partially — it's a mild diuretic)
• If your pee is dark yellow → drink more
• If it's pale/clear → you're good
```

## PART 6: Simple Rules (The TL;DR)

## 49.15 The 10 rules

```
1. Eat real food (if it grew or had a mother, it's real)
2. Half your plate = vegetables (any kind, any cooking method)
3. Protein at every meal (keeps you full, repairs your body)
4. Drink water (2-3 litres/day — keep a bottle visible)
5. Don't drink your calories (no soda, no juice, no sugary coffee)
6. Eat breakfast (even if small — breaks the fast, stabilises blood sugar)
7. Prep on Sunday (2 hours saves 5+ hours during the week)
8. 80/20 rule (eat well most of the time, enjoy treats without guilt)
9. No food is "evil" — portion and frequency matter
10. Sleep 7-8 hours (sleep affects hunger hormones more than any diet)
```

## Summary

✅ Macronutrients: protein (builds), carbs (energy), fat (hormones + brain)
✅ The plate model: 1/2 veggies, 1/4 protein, 1/4 carbs
✅ Developer traps: energy drinks, desk eating, skipping meals, late-night snacking
✅ Meal prep: batch cook Sunday, eat all week (2 proteins + 2 carbs + 3 veggies)
✅ Zero-effort meals: yogurt + fruit, eggs + toast, rotisserie chicken + microwave rice
✅ Brain foods: fatty fish, nuts, berries, dark chocolate, leafy greens
✅ Hydration: 2-3L water, visible bottle, pale pee = good
✅ Budget: eggs, oats, rice, beans, frozen veg, canned fish ($30-50/week)
✅ 80/20 rule: consistent good habits beat perfect short-lived diets

## Key takeaway

**Eating healthy is a system, not willpower.** You don't need discipline to eat well if the good food is already prepared and the bad food isn't in your house. Meal prep is the cheat code — one decision on Sunday eliminates 21 food decisions during the week. That's engineering applied to nutrition.
